# Jasmine Kamara

Nanga def! That's hello in Wolof, my native language. I'm Jasmine (JK).

I'm from southern California, so I'm usually somewhere near a beach. I'm West African, Gambian and Sierra Leonean, and I'm finishing my degrees in Computer Science and in Science, Technology and Society at Brown.

Most of my work sits where data, design and research overlap. I've built a handbook that helps CS instructors teach responsible technology, helped a food app grow, modeled how electric vehicles affect the climate, and looked at how technology spreads risk unevenly. I also TA CS0111, the responsible tech course, and wrote two of its labs.

I've studied abroad in Ghana, Mexico and Indonesia. Outside of work I'm hiking, reading or listening to music, and there's a good chance I'm thinking about cheesecake. It's been my birthday dessert for seven years straight.

This repo is the code for my portfolio site (Next.js + TypeScript). If you want to run it, see [docs/DEVELOPMENT.md](docs/DEVELOPMENT.md).
